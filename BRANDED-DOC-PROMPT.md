Create a polished, branded client document from the attached OVERVIEW.md and screenshots. The document is a free site audit and proposal from Integrate (Jack) to Craig, owner of **Prestige Cheshire Detailing**, a mobile machine-polishing and Gyeon ceramic-coating specialist covering Chester, Northwich, Winsford, Sandbach, Crewe, Nantwich, Macclesfield, Wilmslow and Bramhall.

## Brand (match the new website exactly)
- Page background: black `#08080A`. Panels and cards: `#0F1013`, plus `#17181C` for raised elements.
- Main text: chrome white `#F2F1ED`. Secondary text: `#A3A5AB`. Small labels: `#6B6D73`.
- One accent only: logo red `#E3120B` (use `#FF2A1F` sparingly for emphasis). Use red for a thin horizon line under headings, key numbers and the call to action. Never use it for large fills.
- Hairline dividers: `rgba(242,241,237,0.12)`.
- Headlines: **Archivo**, widest width (wdth 125), weight 800, UPPERCASE, tight letter-spacing. Body: Archivo regular at normal width. Labels and eyebrows: **JetBrains Mono**, 11px, uppercase, letter-spaced.
- Logo: `mark.svg` (black disc, white car outline, red line beneath) on the cover and in the footer.
- Signature motif: a single thin red horizontal line, like the red line under the car in Craig's logo and a reflection on polished paint. Use it under the cover title and as section dividers.
- Tone of voice: confident, warm, plain English, written to Craig as "you". It should feel like a premium studio talking to a skilled tradesperson it respects. No jargon (write "show up in Google", not "SERP visibility").

## Structure
1. **Cover.** "Prestige Cheshire Detailing: free site audit and homepage concept", prepared by Integrate for Craig, dated 24 November 2026. Use the hero screenshot of the new homepage.
2. **A short personal opening.** Three or four sentences from Jack: we looked at your work, the cars and the 100% recommendation from 21 Facebook reviewers speak for themselves, and here's what's stopping more people finding you.
3. **Your free site audit.** What's working (reputation, the quality of the work, the clear premium niche, WhatsApp already set up). What's holding you back: the "Web space not ready" 404 on prestigecheshiredetailing.co.uk, with the desktop and mobile screenshots; everything living on Facebook; and the separate "Prestige Valeting" site and Trustpilot page with a different phone number. Present that last one as something to check, not an accusation.
4. **How customers find you now, and who they find instead.** The indicative search checks from OVERVIEW.md: TKO Detailing, Cheshire Car Care, and directories like Checkatrade and Bark showing for the searches Craig should own. Keep the method note that these are indicative, not a ranked Google UK report.
5. **What a weak online presence is costing you.** The missed searches, the dead link from Facebook and the name confusion. Include the estimate, labelled clearly as an estimate, with no invented figures.
6. **Quick wins you can do this week.** The five free actions from OVERVIEW.md, as a numbered checklist Craig could tick off.
7. **Your new homepage.** A before and after: the 404 screenshots next to the new desktop and mobile screenshots. Explain the concept (the red line from his logo), the Polish Pass with its swirl-finder light (use the torch screenshot), and how every section leads to a WhatsApp message.
8. **How we fix the rest.** Four blocks, each tied to a specific problem found:
   - Full website build, hosting and edits → fixes the 404
   - SEO automations (town and service pages, content engine, schema, Google Business Profile posts, rank tracking, monthly report) → wins "ceramic coating Chester" and similar searches
   - Review automation (NFC/QR plates, automatic requests) → moves the 100% reputation onto Google
   - AI lead capture (instant replies, website assistant, WhatsApp agent that qualifies and books) → no enquiry missed while Craig is polishing
9. **What changes in the first 90 days.** A simple three-step timeline (month 1: site live and Google profile sorted; month 2: town and service pages and review plates; month 3: content engine and lead agent running, first monthly report). Describe what Craig will *see*, without promising rankings or numbers.
10. **Next step: book a call with Jack.** One clear red call-to-action block.

## Rules
- Back every claim with a finding from OVERVIEW.md. Don't add facts, statistics, reviews, quotes or competitor details that aren't in it.
- Label any estimate clearly as an estimate. No invented numbers, no guarantees, and no promised rankings or revenue.
- **No prices anywhere.** Pricing is only discussed on Jack's call.
- Keep it readable for a busy business owner: short paragraphs, generous spacing, one idea per page section, screenshots large.
- It should read like a premium studio's audit and proposal: generous enough to be valuable on its own even if Craig never signs, and persuasive enough that choosing Integrate for the full build feels like the obvious next step.
