# Prestige Cheshire Detailing: creative direction

## Concept (one sentence)
The red line under the car in Craig's logo becomes the horizon of the whole site: it's the reflection running along a freshly polished panel, and it leads the visitor from dull, swirled paint to deep gloss and then into a WhatsApp chat with Craig.

## What we took from the benchmark (1aim.vercel.app)
- **Cinematic darkness with one hot accent.** Near-black ground, a single red used sparingly, and photography lit like a studio. We take the *restraint*. Their red is one of several accents; ours is one line.
- **Wide, engineered display type.** 1aim uses a squared techno face. We use Archivo at its widest setting (125) for an automotive, coachbuilt feel, set against a mono for spec labels. The faces are different, and so is the rhythm.
- **A curated "showroom" of vehicles.** 1aim uses a card grid. We turn it into a pinned horizontal passage ("The Garage") of Craig's *real* jobs, with oversized model names, and no cards.
- **A giant wordmark at the close.** We keep the confidence but make it ours: an outlined PRESTIGE with the red line drawn under it, like the logo.
- **Not taken:** their hexagon pattern, curved carousel, AI-rendered supercars and Lamborghini palette. Every car shown here is a real customer car from Craig's own photos.

## Feeling down the scroll
- **Top: calm, expensive, instantly clear.** Beaded water on black paint, a red light streak, and one line that says what they do, where, and how to book.
- **Middle: understanding, then desire.** "Most paint isn't dull, it's scratched." Then the signature polish pass, then the garage of real Range Rovers, Porsches, BMWs and the RS-badged Audi estate.
- **End: trust, then action.** Vans, coverage map, 100% recommend, objections answered, then the studio stage with the red line, where the form becomes a WhatsApp message.

## Storyboard
1. **Header (fixed):** vector logo mark, wordmark, and a "WhatsApp Craig" pill that's always visible. On mobile, a thumb bar with WhatsApp and Call is always visible.
2. **Hero:** AI macro of beaded paint (atmosphere only, and labelled as such in the overview). A specular highlight follows the cursor. Headline: "Paint you can see yourself in." Sub-line covers services, areas, mobile, own water and power, and appointment only. Primary: WhatsApp. Secondary: the phone number in full. Proof chip: 100% recommend · 21 Facebook reviews. A real Porsche Macan photo sits in a tilted frame with a light sweep.
3. **Statement (scrubbed word reveal):** why paint looks dull (swirl marks) and what Craig does about it.
4. **Signature moment, "The Polish Pass":** a pinned scene on Craig's real photo of a black Range Rover panel. A procedurally drawn veil of swirl marks covers it. As you scroll, a rotary polisher pad runs overlapping passes across the panel and erases the swirls, revealing the true reflection underneath. A live counter runs "Swirls removed 0 → 100%" and the stages advance: Wash & decontaminate → Machine polish → Gyeon ceramic coating. Scrolling back puts the swirls back. Nobody in local detailing shows the job *happening* like this.
5. **The Garage (horizontal passage):** real jobs at full height with oversized model names: Porsche Macan, Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, Audi estate, BMW M2, BMW 3 Series, BMW X3.
6. **Services, "Two things, done properly":** machine polishing and Gyeon ceramic coatings (most jobs use the 5-year coating). Cars, vans, motorbikes.
7. **Vans:** "Your van is your shop window." The black VW Transporter, plus Transits.
8. **Coverage:** we bring the water and the power. An SVG map of the nine towns plotted geographically, with the red line drawing a route between them on scroll.
9. **Proof, "The name in every review":** a 100% count-up, 21 Facebook reviews, and the three things reviewers say about Craig, written as themes, not invented quotes, with a link to the real reviews.
10. **Questions (objections):** price, booking, water and power, areas, vans and bikes, how long coatings last.
11. **Final call to action (climax):** the studio stage with the red neon horizon. "Let's get it booked in." A three-field builder (vehicle / service / town) composes a WhatsApp message. Call and email sit alongside.
12. **Footer:** the giant outlined wordmark with the red line, contact details and towns.

## Conversion path
- **Persuaded:** the hero promise, the polish pass (the visible result), and the garage (proof of premium clientele).
- **Objections answered:** price (photos on WhatsApp get a quote), trust (100% of 21 reviewers recommend, Craig named), timing (appointment only, so ask for dates), area (nine towns mapped), logistics (own water and power).
- **Act points:** the header pill (always), the mobile thumb bar (always), the hero CTAs, after the polish pass, after the garage, after vans, the coverage map, the proof section, the FAQ and the final builder. Every one opens WhatsApp or the phone. Nothing is hidden behind animation.
