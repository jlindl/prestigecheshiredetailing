# Direction: Prestige Cheshire Detailing, demo v2

**Concept:** A showroom finish, delivered to your driveway. Calm, confident and editorial, the way a premium coating brand would present itself, not a flashy template.

**Why a v2:** Craig's digital marketer preferred the simpler, cleaner demo built by another developer (dark, uppercase grotesk, full-bleed RS3, plain sections). This version keeps that restraint and structure but raises the craft: better typography, real content in every block, smoother motion, his preferred map, and a booking ending that actually converts.

**Taken from the reference demo (techniques, not the look):** full-bleed hero on Craig's own RS3, uppercase grotesk headings with small red eyebrows, services as three image-led cards, a recent work gallery, real review quotes, the six-step process, FAQs, "Tell us about your vehicle" as the ending.

**Improved on it:**
- Hero headline set with real typographic contrast; the RS3 is Craig's own photo.
- Review block with no empty card; quotes laid out as a proper editorial wall.
- Recent work auto-scrolls (Craig asked for auto-scroll), tap to enlarge.
- Process steps light up as you scroll past them.
- Coverage map instead of a town list (Craig said the map was better).
- Final section is a 3-tap WhatsApp message builder, on Craig's own photo (the reference used a BMW photo that isn't his).
- Craig's corrections applied: "we" voice, no "swirl" wording, no power supplied, no 5-year claim, Cheshire, the North West and North Wales up to Wigan and Manchester.
- Comment mode (`?feedback`) so Craig and his marketer can pin notes.

**Feeling down the scroll:** top, quiet confidence (one car, one line). Middle, proof and process (the work, the reviews, the method). End, easy action (we come to you, here's how to book).

**Signature moment:** the process section. A pinned photo on one side while the six steps light up one by one as you scroll, so the visitor feels the prep that goes in before a coating.

**Conversion path:** WhatsApp in the header, the hero, after services, after reviews, after areas and as the finale; a sticky WhatsApp/Call bar on mobile; objections answered before the end (price, area, water and power, booking, coatings, vans and bikes).
